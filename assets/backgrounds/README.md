Five original AI-generated environments, optimized to WebP for local delivery (no external image requests).

Shared prompt: Premium fantasy game environment beneath a separate air hockey table, overhead camera, cinematic lighting, detailed edges, calm dark center, no table, characters, UI, text, borders or logos.

- classic: luxurious teal and gold arcade courtyard with plants and waterfalls.
- space: blue-purple nebula, distant planets and asteroids.
- ice: crystalline glacial lake, turquoise ice and snowy cliffs.
- desert: amber dunes and ancient sandstone temple ruins.
- wind: emerald floating cliffs, foliage, waterfalls and pale jade cloud ribbons.
